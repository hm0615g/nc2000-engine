## Normalization Rules

- All 198 catalog sets have maximum non-Attack EVs and maximum non-Attack
  DVs subject to Hidden Power constraints.
- Sets that use Attack get maximum Attack subject to gender and Hidden Power
  constraints. Other sets, including special Hidden Power and fixed-damage
  users, get `evs.atk = 0` and the minimum Attack DV preserving maximum HP,
  non-Attack stats, Hidden Power power, shiny status and required gender.
  Metronome/Mimic/Mirror Move retain Attack for physical moves they may access.
  Female preference is enabled by the
  frozen prior's weighted direct-Attract source mix, except for a set's own
  Attract targets and camouflage constraints. At maximum HP, the usual female
  or genderless minimum is Attack DV `1` (`ivs.atk = 2`); the Attack stat itself
  remains positive.
- In multi-member clusters where a sibling team requires `"M"` on the same
  species (e.g., `Cloyster` with `Explosion` in `vc-kodoku-nash-a`), `"M"` is
  preserved and `ivs.atk = 18` (Gen 2 Attack DV `9`, the lowest male-compatible
  DV for 50/50 gender species) is used so the cluster shares an identical Team
  Preview signature.
- Moves, items, levels and public genders are preserved from the preceding
  catalog. Return/Frustration happiness is corrected to 255/0.
- Differences from the original inventory/evolved inputs:
  - `30` teams updated
  - `74` sets updated
  - `10` gender fields changed to `"F"`

The [training-v3 verification](../training-v3/README.md) independently checks all
distributed spreads and exact catalog support in the prior. It fixes the
special Hidden Power omissions, `pb-100` Rapid Spin Starmie's low Attack DV,
and `pb-13` Porygon2's Frustration happiness. Previous preview computations are
retired and selection uses live search pending recomputation.
